---
title: 'Privacy'
description: 'Browsing data on this static blog.'
---

Comments and analytics scripts are disabled in this version. Your theme preference is stored in this browser under `blog.tech.theme` and can be cleared in browser settings.

Requests still pass through the hosting provider. No login is required, and search queries are not sent to a local LLM. Full-text search reads this site’s static index in your browser.
